How to connect your router

Simple idea: this website gives your MikroTik one script. You paste it into WinBox. The router then calls this website every minute to show that it is connected.
What you need: A MikroTik router, WinBox access, the router login details, and internet access from the router.
1
Fill in this form

Use the same username and password you use to log in with WinBox.

2
Click Generate Script

The router will be created. On the next page, download its WinBox Script.

3
Paste in WinBox

Open WinBox, connect to the router, open Terminal, and paste the whole script.

4
Keep internet working

The router needs outbound internet access. A public IP is not needed.

5
Wait one minute

The first connection happens immediately. The router checks again every minute.

6
Look for Online

Return to Routers and refresh. Green Online means the connection works.

If it stays Offline: check router internet access, the public application URL, and the WinBox Terminal log.
